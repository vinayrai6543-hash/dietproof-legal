Request DietProof Account Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your DietProof account and associated personal information.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open DietProof and sign in.
  2. Open the account settings screen.
  3. Choose the account deletion option.
  4. Follow the confirmation instructions and confirm the deletion request.

The app's deletion workflow is designed to remove your account and personal scan history.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an account-deletion request to our support email.

Email an account-deletion request

Use the email address registered to your DietProof account, and include the account email address in your request so we can identify the account and verify the request if necessary.

Never send your password, authentication codes, or access tokens.

What is deleted?

The in-app deletion workflow is designed to delete your authentication account and personal scan history. Shared product information may be preserved, with personal attribution removed where supported by the database cleanup process.

If you submit a request by email, it is a request for support to process; sending the email does not itself delete your account. We may contact you to verify account ownership before proceeding.

What may be retained?

Shared product information that does not identify you may be retained. Some technical logs, backups, or records held by third-party service providers may be subject to their own retention practices or applicable legal requirements.
